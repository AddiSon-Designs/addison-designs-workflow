Privacy policy

AddiSon Designs Invoice Auto
Updated November 1, 2026

This policy describes the AddiSon Designs shop workflow and its Google Drive invoice integration. The integration is operated by AddiSon Designs for authorized shop staff to send invoices and archive invoice PDFs.

Information processed

The workflow processes customer and contact names, email addresses, phone numbers, order details, invoice numbers and dates, line items, quantities, prices, and payment status. Invoice PDFs contain the details displayed on the invoice.

Google Drive access and use

The connected Google account grants the integration the Google Drive permission shown on Google's authorization screen. The current permission permits broad Drive access. The implemented invoice archive uses that access to locate and create year and month folders under the configured Invoices folder, find previously archived invoices, and create or update invoice PDF files. It does not need the contents of unrelated Drive documents for this workflow.

Google access and refresh tokens are used on the server to authenticate these requests. The integration saves the same PDF attached to an invoice email after the email provider confirms sending. An archive-only action can save an invoice without emailing it again.

Storage and service providers

Workflow data is stored using Supabase, and archived PDFs are stored in the connected Google Drive account. Invoice emails, including their PDF attachments and a shop copy, are processed by Resend. The web interface is hosted on GitHub Pages. These providers process the data required to deliver their respective services. Access to archived PDFs also follows the connected Drive account's existing sharing settings.

Retention and removal

Archived PDFs remain in Google Drive until an authorized account user removes them. Removing a PDF does not automatically remove its invoice record or previously delivered emails. Contact AddiSon Designs to request correction or deletion of workflow records. Revoking Google access stops future archive operations but does not delete existing PDFs or invoice emails.

Access controls

The workflow requires a shop account. Invoice email and archive actions require an administrator profile, and the archive checks the shop's organization against its configured organization. Google credentials are stored as server-side secrets, not in the public application source.

Your Google account

You can review or revoke the integration's access through your Google Account's third-party connections settings. Reauthorization may be needed after access is revoked or expires.

Google user data

Google user data is used to provide the invoice archiving function described here. The integration's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
