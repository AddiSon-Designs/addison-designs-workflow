Terms of use

AddiSon Designs Invoice Auto
Updated November 1, 2026

These terms describe use of the AddiSon Designs shop workflow and AddiSon Designs Invoice Auto integration by authorized shop staff. This application supports internal order management, invoice delivery, and Google Drive invoice archiving.

Authorized use

Use an account provided or approved by AddiSon Designs. Only connect a Google account whose owner has authorized access to the invoice destination. Keep account credentials and Google authorization tokens private.

Invoice operations

The Send action emails the invoice to its selected recipient, includes a shop copy, and attempts to save the PDF in Google Drive. A separate PDF-save action archives the invoice without sending another email. Review customer details, recipients, prices, totals, and dates before sending.

Archive organization

The archive stores invoices under the configured Invoices folder using the invoice creation year and a two-digit month, 01 through 12. Filenames use the customer name and invoice number. Saving the same invoice again normally updates its existing archive file.

Delivery and availability

Email delivery and Drive archiving are separate operations. An email may succeed while archiving fails. Check the displayed status and use the PDF-only retry when appropriate. Authorization expiry, connectivity, folder permissions, and provider outages can interrupt service.

Privacy and access

The Privacy policy explains data processing and Google Drive access. A Google account owner may revoke access through Google Account settings. Revocation does not remove previously stored invoices.

Scope

These application-use terms do not replace a customer's order agreement or establish separate apparel pricing, payment, shipping, or return policies. Contact AddiSon Designs with questions about account access or an invoice.
